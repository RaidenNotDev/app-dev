# 🌟 Twinkling Watermelon

**Twinkling Watermelon** is a *South Korean drama* that combines **music**, **youth**, and **family**.
It tells the story of *Ha Eun Gyeol*, a musical genius who travels back in time and meets his father as a teenager.



## 🎸 Main Characters
**Ha Eun Gyeol** – A talented student who loves music.
**Ha Yi Chan** – Eun Gyeol’s father in his youth, full of dreams.
**Choi Se Kyung** – The cello goddess admired by many.
**Yoon Cheong Ah** – A quiet girl who communicates through sign language.



## 💫 Why I Like It

> The story is emotional and inspiring.
> It shows how **music connects generations** and heals relationships.



## 🎶 Favorite Quote

> “Even if our worlds sound different, our hearts beat to the same rhythm.”
